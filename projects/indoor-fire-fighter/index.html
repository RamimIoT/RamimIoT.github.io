Indoor Fire Fighter System (ESP32 + IoT Cloud)

A smart device that detects and extinguishes indoor fires. It vents flammable gas, detects flames, aims a water jet at the fire and raises the alarm locally and through the Arduino IoT Cloud, all driven by an ESP32 Dev Kit v1 with battery backup.

How it works

  • Gas detection & ventilation: an MQ2 gas sensor senses flammable gases, and an exhaust fan extracts them.
  • Flame detection: two flame sensors detect a fire.
  • Suppression: a water pump starts the flow of water, and a servo attached to the pump pipe directs it toward the fire.
  • Alarm: a buzzer sounds a fire alarm, and the alert is also sent to users through Arduino IoT Cloud.
  • Remote dashboard: users can monitor and control the system from the cloud dashboard.
