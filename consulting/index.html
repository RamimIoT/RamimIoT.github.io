02 · Core expertise

Four areas where I make the biggest difference

End-to-end IoT

One owner from the sensor to the decision

Most IoT projects fail at the seams: hardware that the firmware can't drive reliably, or data the platform can't trust. I design all of it together, so each layer is built for the next.

  • Sense: sensors, signal conditioning and enclosures chosen for the real site, not the lab.
  • Decide: rules and logic at the edge or in the cloud, with safe fallbacks when the network drops.
  • Act: relays, pumps and aerators switched with acknowledgements, so you know it really happened.

Multi-tenant architecture

Many customers, one platform, each one's data kept apart

I architected and built SmartSense, a multi-tenant IoT platform for every sensor and actuator product at SmartData Technologies. I bring the same principles to your platform.

  • Isolation in four layers: per-organisation broker ACLs, server-verified sessions, PostgreSQL row-level security and composite foreign keys.
  • Proven by tests: negative tests that try to cross tenant boundaries, so isolation is checked, not assumed.
  • Operator features: sites and devices, live updates, drag-and-drop dashboards, alerts and immutable daily reports.

Industrial automation

Retrofit the plant you have, automate it safely

I connect existing controllers, meters and machines to modern monitoring and control, without ripping out working equipment.

  • Protocols: Modbus RTU/TCP over RS-485 and BACnet, with sensor nodes hardened for high-interference factory floors.
  • Safe control: hysteresis, cooldowns, paired rules and "ask a person first" approvals before anything switches.
  • Reconciliation: compares what automation intended with what the relay reported, and corrects drift after power cuts.
  • Use cases: predictive maintenance, production and OEE monitoring, energy and aeration control.

Secure data pipelines

Telemetry you can trust, from device to report

A pipeline is only as good as its weakest hop. I design each one so data is authenticated at the edge, survives outages and stays private in storage.

  • At the edge: scoped credentials and ACLs that lock each customer to its own topics, with TLS where the deployment needs it.
  • No silent data loss: persistent broker sessions, QoS and a raw capture store you can replay.
  • In storage: time-series databases with retention, tenant-scoped access and routine backups.
  • For people: live dashboards, threshold alerts and immutable daily PDF reports for audits.

07 · FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes. I have worked remotely with international clients since 2021. Design, firmware and platform work is fully remote, and hardware can be built and tested in Bangladesh and shipped, or sourced in your country.

Do you only advise, or do you build too?

Both. I can review and plan, or design the PCB, write the firmware, build the platform and support the field deployment myself.

Can you work with our existing devices or cloud?

Yes. I have integrated existing controllers over Modbus and BACnet, extend or fix existing firmware, and connect to the broker or cloud you already use instead of forcing a rebuild.

How is a project priced?

After the discovery call I send a written scope with a fixed quote per milestone. Open-ended work such as research or rescue can run on time and materials instead.

Will you sign an NDA, and who owns the work?

Yes, I'm happy to sign an NDA before we discuss details. Ownership of designs and source code is agreed in the proposal; by default, deliverables are handed over to you once paid.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of the problem, where the devices will run, roughly how many you need, and your timeline. Use the form below and I'll reply with a time for the discovery call.
